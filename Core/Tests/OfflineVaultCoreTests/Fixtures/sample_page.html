Water purification in the field

Boiling is the most reliable way to make water safe to drink. Bring water to a rolling boil for at least one minute; at altitudes above 2,000 metres boil for three minutes.

Chemical treatment with unscented household bleach: add 2 drops of 6% bleach per litre of clear water, stir, and wait 30 minutes before drinking. Double the dose for cloudy water.

Filters rated to 0.2 microns remove bacteria and protozoa but not viruses; combine a filter with chemical treatment where viruses are a concern.
